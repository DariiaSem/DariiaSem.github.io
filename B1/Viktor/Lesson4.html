Task 07

Grammar: Comparatives

We use comparatives to highlight the difference between two people, places, or things.

Short Adjectives

Add -er to one-syllable words (and two-syllable words ending in y).

  • Cheap -> Cheaper
  • Large -> Larger
  • Busy -> Busier (drop the y, add ier)

Lviv is cheaper than Geneva.

Long Adjectives

Use more before words with two or more syllables.

  • Modern -> More modern
  • Crowded -> More crowded
  • Picturesque -> More picturesque

Berlin is more crowded than Lviv.

Task 09

Grammar: Superlatives

We use superlatives to declare that something represents the highest degree within a group.

The Rules and Irregulars

Always use the before a superlative. Add -est to short words. Use most before long words.

Short Cheap -> The cheapest
Busy -> The busiest
Long Modern -> The most modern
Crowded -> The most crowded
Irregular Good -> Better -> The best
Bad -> Worse -> The worst

Task 12

Writing Strategy

When writing a comparison, you must use linking words to connect your ideas smoothly.

To show difference

  • › However,
  • › On the other hand,
  • › Whereas (joins two parts of a sentence)

Lviv is historic, whereas Berlin is very modern.

To show similarity

  • › Similarly,
  • › Likewise,
  • › Both cities...

Similarly, the hospitals in both cities are well equipped.
