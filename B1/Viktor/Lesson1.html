Task 04

The Rules: Simple vs. Continuous

Present Simple

  • Routines and habits (I check the monitors every 5 minutes.)
  • Permanent situations (I work in the NHS.)
  • State verbs (love, know, believe, understand) (I need a scalpel.)

Present Continuous

  • Actions happening right now (The patient is waking up.)
  • Temporary situations (I am working the night shift this week.)
  • Trends (Our clinic is seeing more trauma cases lately.)

Task 06

Lexicon Hub

Explore essential phrases for introducing yourself and medical routines.

1. Handover
2. On Call
3. Approachable
4. Vitals

Handover (Noun)

Meaning: The process of transferring patient care from one doctor/nurse to another at the end of a shift.

"Hi, nice to meet you. I'm ready for the morning handover whenever you are."

On Call (Phrase)

Meaning: Available to be called into the hospital for work, usually during the night or weekend.

"I can't go to the pub tonight; I am on call until 6 AM."

Approachable (Adjective)

Meaning: Friendly and easy to talk to (essential for good first impressions!).

"The new Head of Anesthesia is very approachable; you can ask her anything."

Vitals (Noun, plural)

Meaning: Vital signs (pulse, blood pressure, respiration rate, temperature).

"I am checking the patient's vitals before we administer the propofol."
