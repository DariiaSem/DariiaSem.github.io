Course Plan for an Anesthesiologist

Topic Balance

70% Daily life, travel, culture 30% Anesthesiology & clinical English

3 of every 10 lessons are medical; the rest cover hobbies, relationships, culture, and travel scenarios — medicine is trained as one life topic among many, not the syllabus's spine.

Skill Coverage

Every lesson deliberately foregrounds one or two skills, so listening, speaking, reading, writing and grammar are all trained in rotation across the 30 lessons rather than repeating the same lesson shape.

Grammar Arc

Because conditionals were an unknown gap, the whole course is threaded around building them one at a time — Zero → First → Second → Third → Mixed — alongside the tenses, reported speech, passive voice and formal register he needs for conferences.
