Course Overview

Built on the same architecture as the Executive Syllabus

This course keeps the 50/50 split and the C1 grammar arc that worked well for the corporate-banking track, re-platformed for a software developer's world. Odd lessons dig into the profession — architecture, ethics, incident response, startup culture, AI's impact on the job. Even lessons stay deliberately career-agnostic: philosophy, psychology, cinema, sport, and — where it's genuinely useful rather than a gimmick — the lived experience of relocating from Ukraine to Spain.

Grammar is not front-loaded: each lesson's structure should be discovered through the reading and case material first, then consolidated. The three review checkpoints (after L10, L20, and L30) audit tense accuracy, prepositional precision, and complex syntax in that order — mirroring the original course's escalation from baseline accuracy → structural mastery → abstraction and fluency.

Tech Track (15)

Software architecture, AI & automation, cybersecurity, startup/corporate culture, incident response, tech ethics.

Social Track (15)

Philosophy, psychology, cinema, sport, and identity — occasionally threaded with expat life in Spain, never forced.
