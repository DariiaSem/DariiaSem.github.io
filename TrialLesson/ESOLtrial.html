Tasks 5 & 6

Reading & Lexical Diagnostics 📖

Please read the following academic paragraph aloud. Then, answer the diagnostic questions below.

"When cartoonist Charles M. Schulz was a boy in elementary school, other boys teased him for being small and not very good at sports, and his art teacher told him he had no talent for drawing. He had few friends and was too shy to talk to a red-haired girl he admired. Later in life, Schulz used his childhood experiences in his comic strip Peanuts: the strip's main character, the sad and lonely Charlie Brown, represents Schulz as a little boy. Peanuts was unique at the time because it contained no adult characters. Readers fell in love with Charlie Brown, and Peanuts eventually became one of the most popular comic strips of all time."
Task 5: Main Idea Assessment

What is the main idea of the passage?

  • A. Peanuts was the world's most widely read comic strip.
  • B. Schulz was a very famous cartoonist.
  • C. Schulz turned the pain of his youth into success as an adult.
  • D. The comic strip Peanuts featured children as its only characters.
Task 6: Inference & Lexical Diagnosis

Based on the text, define the words "admired" and "unique" in your own words. Secondly, how might the absence of adult characters explain why readers fell in love with Charlie Brown?

Task 8: Sentence Combination

Read the two sentences and choose the best way of combining them:
Wolves travel in packs. They are rarely spotted alone.

A. Wolves are rarely spotted alone if they travel in packs.
B. Because they travel in packs, wolves are rarely spotted alone.
C. After traveling in packs, wolves are rarely spotted alone.
D. Wolves are rarely spotted alone, although they travel in packs.

Why are the other options structurally or logically incorrect?
