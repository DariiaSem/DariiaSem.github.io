Task 4

Advanced Syntax: Mixed Conditionals & Inversion 🧠

Elevating your sentence structures for formal negotiations and professional emphasis.

Structure Function Example
Mixed Conditional
(Past & Present)		 Linking a past action to a current ongoing result in business. "If we had secured that merger last quarter, our market share would be much larger today."
Inversion
(Conditionals)		 Highly formal alternative to 'If' (common in legal/finance). "Had I known about the compliance issue, I would have delayed the launch." (Instead of: If I had known...)
Negative Inversion Adding strong emphasis to a restriction or rare event. "Under no circumstances should this data be leaked to the press."

Your Turn: Complete the Corporate Thoughts ✍️

Inversion (Past Condition):

Negative Inversion (Emphasis):

Rarely do we see [finish the sentence] in the current financial climate.

Task 5

High-Stakes Strategy 🤔

Use advanced hypotheticals to justify your corporate strategy.

1. The Client Dilemma

Would you rather maintain a toxic but highly lucrative client relationship OR cut ties and lose 20% of your annual revenue?

2. The Management Crisis

Would you rather inherit an underperforming team with great attitudes OR a high-performing team of absolute narcissists?

3. The PR Nightmare

If a mistake was made, would you rather take full public responsibility for a subordinate's massive error OR throw them under the bus to save the firm's reputation?

8. Idioms in the Boardroom

What do the bold phrases mean?

  • "Let's get down to brass tacks." (Focus on the essential details).
  • "I'm just playing devil's advocate here, but..." (Arguing against an idea for the sake of debate/testing it).
  • "We need to cut through the red tape to expedite this loan." (Bureaucracy/Administrative obstacles).
  • "Their tech sector is really ahead of the curve." (More advanced or innovative than competitors).
