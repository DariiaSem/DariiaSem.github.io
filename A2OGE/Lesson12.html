Task 10 · Grammar Theory 1

So vs. Such

We use so and such to mean 'very' or to add extreme emphasis to a statement.

📈 So + Adjective / Adverb

Use so directly before describing words.

✅ "The video was so funny."
✅ "She speaks so quickly."
🎁 Such (a/an) + Adjective + Noun

Use such when there is a noun at the end of the phrase.

✅ "It was such a funny video."
✅ "They are such famous influencers." (No 'a/an' because it is plural).
🖊️

Task 12 · Grammar Theory 2

Relative Clauses (Non-Defining)

Last lesson we learned how to connect sentences. Now, let's look at adding extra, non-essential information using commas.

📝 The "Extra Info" Comma Rule

If we already know exactly who/what we are talking about (like a name), the relative clause is just "extra info". We MUST use commas. We CANNOT use "that".

✅ "My brother, who hates social media, never takes photos."
(I only have one brother, so you know who I mean. The clause is just extra detail).
❌ "My brother, that hates social media, never takes photos." (Never use 'that' with commas).
🔗 Full-Clause "Which"

Sometimes, which doesn't refer to a single noun, but to the entire previous situation.

✅ "His video went viral, which was a big surprise."
(The surprise was the whole situation of going viral).
