Task 2 · Vocabulary Discovery

Routines & Productivity

⏰

1. Alarm

An electronic sound or device designed to wake you up or give a warning.

I forgot to set my alarm and woke up late.

🚇

2. Commute

The regular journey between your home and your place of work or study.

My morning commute takes about 45 minutes by train.

🧽

3. Chore

A routine task, especially a household one like cleaning or washing dishes.

Doing the laundry is my least favorite chore.

🔁

4. Habit

Something that you do often and regularly, sometimes without knowing that you are doing it.

Biting your nails is a bad habit.

🚀

5. Productivity

The rate at which a person does useful work.

Listening to focus music increases my productivity.

⏳

6. Procrastinate

To delay doing something that you should do, usually because you don't want to do it.

Stop trying to procrastinate and just finish your essay!

Task 7 · Grammar Theory 1

Prepositions of Time: In, On, At

These small words are easy to confuse. Memorize these specific rules.

PrepositionUsed ForExamples
IN Months, Years, Seasons, Parts of the day in July, in 2026, in summer, in the morning/afternoon
ON Days of the week, Specific dates on Monday, on Friday morning, on the 5th of May, on my birthday
AT Exact times, Night, Holidays, The weekend at 5:00 AM, at night, at noon, at Christmas, at the weekend (UK)

Task 9 · Grammar Theory 2

Phrasal Verbs (Productivity & Routines)

Native speakers use these phrasal verbs when talking about work and daily tasks.

Put off

To delay doing something (procrastinate).

"I keep putting off the report until tomorrow."

Wrap up

To finish a task or a meeting.

"Let's wrap up this meeting so we can go home."

Catch up

To do work you didn't have time to do earlier.

"I need to catch up on my emails this weekend."

Burn out

To become extremely exhausted from working too hard.

"If you don't take a break, you will burn out."
