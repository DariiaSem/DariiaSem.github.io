Task 7 · Grammar Theory

Present Simple vs Continuous

Present Simple (Regular, Facts, "Every day")

V1 (or V1 + s for He/She/It).

✅ I buy coffee every morning.

Present Continuous (Right now, "At the moment")

am/is/are + V(ing).

✅ I am buying coffee right now.

✅

Task 9 · Grammar Theory

Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Past Simple (Finished Time, "Yesterday, in 2020")

V2 (or didn't + V1).

✅ I broke my leg in 2015. (It is finished.)

Present Perfect (Experience, Result Now, "Ever, Just, Already")

have/has + V3.

✅ I have broken my leg. (It is broken now. Result.)

✅ Have you ever been to Paris?

✅
