Task 9 · Grammar Theory

Zero Conditional (Facts)

We use the Zero Conditional to talk about facts and things that are always true.

Formula: Facts & Rules
IF + Present Simple
➡️
Present Simple
✅ "If you heat ice, it melts."
✅ "If people travel, they learn new things."
📐
Task 10 · Grammar Theory

First Conditional (Future)

We use the First Conditional to talk about real plans and things that can happen in the future.

Formula: Future Possibility
IF + Present Simple
➡️
WILL + Verb
✅ "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at the hotel."
✅ "If we do not hurry, we will miss our flight!"
🖊️

Task 16 · Pronunciation

Voice Intonation

When you use IF, your voice goes UP in the middle, and DOWN at the end. Listen and repeat.

If we go to Rome ↗️,
we will eat pizza ↘️.

If you miss the flight ↗️,
you will cry ↘️.
