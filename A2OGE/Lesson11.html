Task 3: Word 1

The Main Event — Headliner

Meaning
  • If a band plays at 2:00 PM when nobody is there, are they the headliner?
  • Is this the most famous artist whose name is printed in the largest letters on the poster? (Yes!)
Form
Reveal
Noun (Countable)

Task 6: Grammar Theory

Relative Clauses

We use relative clauses to connect two sentences and give more information about a noun without repeating it.

PronounUsed ForExample from Text
Who / That People "The people who attend Glastonbury..."
Which / That Things / Animals "Festivals that take place in the UK..."
Where Places "The stage where they played was huge."
Whose Possession (his/her/its) "A band whose songs I listened to..."
🎯 Quick Practice: Combine these sentences: "Adele is a singer. She played an amazing encore."
→ Adele is the singer played an amazing encore.
Task 7: Grammar Game

The Time Machine: Past vs. Used To

Rule Clarification: A very common mistake is saying "I used to went". Remember:
For a past habit (many times): Subject + used to + Base Verb (V1). Example: I used to go.
For a single finished action: Subject + Past Simple (V2). Example: I went.

🎸

When I was a teenager, I ______ to rock concerts every weekend.

Task 8: Role-Play

Lost in the Crowd

You are at a massive music festival. You went to get drinks and now you can't find your friend. You call them on your phone. Fill in the blanks with relative pronouns (who, which, where) or festival vocab!

Friend
Hello?! It's so loud! [Where / Which] are you?
You
I'm lost! I am standing next to the stage [where / who] the indie band is playing.
Friend
I can't hear you! The [atmosphere / venue] here is crazy. I am near the food trucks.
You
Okay! Wait for me next to the man [which / who] is wearing the giant neon hat!
