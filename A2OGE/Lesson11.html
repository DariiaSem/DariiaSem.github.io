Task 3 · Vocabulary Discovery

Upgrading your Music Words

"Song" and "Singer" are A2 words. Let's look at B1+ festival vocabulary.

🌟

1. Headliner

The main and most famous performer at a concert or festival.

Coldplay is the headliner for Saturday night.

🏟️

2. Venue

The place where a public event or meeting happens.

This club is my favorite music venue in the city.

✨

3. Atmosphere

The feeling, emotion, or mood of a place.

The atmosphere in the crowd was absolutely electric.

👏

4. Encore

An extra song played at the end of a show because the crowd demands it.

They came back to the stage to play an encore.

🎸

5. Gig

An informal word for a live musical performance.

My friend's band has a gig at the pub tonight.

💽

6. Genre

A specific style or category of art, music, or literature.

Rock, jazz, and hip-hop are different musical genres.

Task 8 · Grammar Theory 1

Relative Clauses

We use relative clauses to connect two sentences and give more information about a noun without repeating it.

PronounUsed ForExample from Text
Who / That People "The people who attend Glastonbury..."
Which / That Things / Animals "Festivals that take place in the UK..."
Where Places "The stage where they played was huge."
Whose Possession (his/her/its) "A band whose songs I listened to..."

Task 10 · Grammar Theory 2

"Used To" vs. Past Simple

A very common mistake is confusing a past habit with a single event.

🔄 Used To + Base Verb

For things that happened regularly in the past, but are NOT true now.

✅ "I used to go to festivals every summer." (But now I don't).
❌ "I used to went to festivals." (NEVER use V2 after 'used to').
⚡ Past Simple (V2)

For a single, finished action that happened once.

✅ "Last night, we went to a great gig."
❌ "Last night, we used to go to a gig."
