Task 2 · Vocabulary Discovery

The Language of Sci-Fi

🧠

1. AI (Artificial Intelligence)

Computer systems able to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Many companies use AI to analyze big data.

🤖

2. Robot

A machine capable of carrying out a complex series of actions automatically.

The factory replaced half its workers with robots.

🛸

3. Spaceship

A vehicle designed for travel or operation in outer space.

The astronauts boarded the spaceship for the Mars mission.

👽

4. Alien

A hypothetical or fictional being from another world.

The movie is about an alien invasion on Earth.

🔮

5. Predict

To say or estimate that a specified thing will happen in the future.

It is impossible to accurately predict the weather a month in advance.

🥽

6. Virtual Reality (VR)

A computer-generated simulation of a 3D image or environment.

We will be playing games in complete virtual reality soon.

Task 7 · Grammar Theory 1

Future Continuous

We use the Future Continuous to talk about actions that will be in progress at a specific time in the future.

⏳ will be + V(ing)

Use it when you want to emphasize that an action will be ongoing, not just a single finished event.

👉 "This time next year, I will be living in London."
👉 "In 2050, robots will be driving our cars."
❌ NOT: In 2050, robots will driving our cars.

Task 9 · Grammar Theory 2

The Conditionals Review

Let's review the difference between Zero, First, and Second conditionals.

TypeFormulaUsage & Example
0 (Zero) If + Present, ... Present Facts / General truths.
"If you heat ice, it melts."
1 (First) If + Present, ... Will + V Real/Likely future situations.
"If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home."
2 (Second) If + Past, ... Would + V Unreal/Imaginary present or future.
"If I had a spaceship, I would fly to the moon."
