Memory Check: Order of Adjectives

Before we describe personalities, let's review how we describe physical objects using the OSASCOMP rule (Opinion, Size, Age, Shape, Color, Origin, Material, Purpose).

  • 1. He wore a (black / leather / cool) jacket.
    → He wore a jacket.
  • 2. She bought some (white / running / new) shoes.
    → She bought some shoes.
  • 3. It's an (old / ugly / big) car.
    → It's an car.

Task 8: Reading

Introvert vs. Extrovert

Hover over the dotted text to see the Ukrainian translations!

Psychologists often divide people into two main categories. Extroverts are very outgoing; they get their energy from being around others.

Introverts might be shy, but not always. The main difference is that introverts need to be alone to recharge their battery. Most of them are very reliable friends because they are good listeners.

Of course, nobody is perfect. Both types can be stubborn when it comes to changing their habits!

Task 10: System Rules

Modals of Deduction (Present)

We use these modal verbs to make a logical guess about a situation when we don't know the exact truth.

ModalMeaning / CertaintyExample
Must be I am 99% sure it is TRUE. (Logical deduction) He has been working for 14 hours. He must be exhausted.
Might / May / Could be I am 50% sure. It is possible. She isn't answering her phone. She might be in a meeting.
Can't be I am 99% sure it is FALSE. (Impossible) That can't be John's car. His car is red, not black!
❌ Important: We DO NOT use "mustn't be" to mean "I am sure it is not true". We use can't be!

Task 12: Graphology

What Your Handwriting Says

Graphology is the study of handwriting to find out about a person's character. Here are a few basic rules:

Size: If someone writes with very large letters, they must be an outgoing person who loves attention. If the letters are tiny, the person might be very shy and focused.

Pressure: If the pen presses very hard into the paper, the writer can't be a flexible person; they are probably very stubborn. Light pressure shows someone who is relaxed.
