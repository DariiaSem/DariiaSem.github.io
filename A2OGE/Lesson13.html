Task 3 · Vocabulary Discovery

Sports Fashion Basics

Let's look at words we use to talk about clothes, shoes, and style.

👕

1. Cotton

A natural, breathable material made from a plant. Used in regular t-shirts.

🏃

2. Synthetic

An artificial material (like polyester or nylon). Great for sports gear because it dries quickly.

🔥

3. Hype

Extreme excitement or advertising around a new product (like a sneaker drop).

😎

4. Trendy

Very popular at the current moment; fashionable.

🔎
Task 4 · Word Dive 1

Fit vs. Suit

Meaning
  • If shoes are a Size 10 and you are a Size 10, they fit you. (Physical size/shape).
  • If a yellow shirt makes your eyes look great, it suits you. (Style/Appearance).
🚀
Task 5 · Word Dive 2

The Action — Try on

Meaning
  • To put on a piece of clothing in a shop to see if it fits you.
  • Grammar Note: This is a separable phrasal verb. "Try the shoes on" or "Try them on".

Task 8 · Reading

Behind the Scenes: Fast Sportswear

Hover over the dotted text to see the Ukrainian translations!

Sports fashion and sneaker culture have become a massive business. Brands create hype by releasing limited-edition collections.

But the problem with fast fashion is the waste. People buy trendy items, wear them a few times, and throw them away. Synthetic materials do not break down easily in the environment.

Next time you try on a pair of shoes, ask yourself: "Are they just trendy, or do I actually need them?"

Task 10 · Grammar Theory 1

Order of Adjectives

When we use more than one adjective before a noun, we must put them in a specific order. A helpful trick is OSASCOMP.

📏 The OSASCOMP Rule

Opinion, Size, Age, Shape, Color, Origin, Material, Purpose.

Opinion ➔ Size / Age ➔ Color ➔ Material / Purpose
✅ "A cool new black leather basketball shoe."
✅ "An ugly big green cotton shirt."
❌ "A leather new cool black shoe." (Incorrect order).

Note: We rarely use more than 3 adjectives at once in normal speech!

🖊️
Task 11 · Grammar Practice 1

Order the Gear

Put the adjectives in the correct order.

  • 1. He bought a (black / running / expensive) jacket.
    → He bought an jacket.
  • 2. She was wearing (cotton / comfortable / old) sweatpants.
    → She was wearing sweatpants.
  • 3. I need to buy some (white / tennis / new) shoes.
    → I need to buy some shoes.

Task 12 · Grammar Theory 2

So vs. Such (Review)

Let's quickly review how to add extreme emphasis, as seen in Lesson 12.

⚖️ The Difference

So + Adjective (No noun at the end).
Such (a/an) + Adjective + Noun (Must end with a noun).

✅ "These sneakers are so expensive."
✅ "They are such expensive sneakers."
🖊️
