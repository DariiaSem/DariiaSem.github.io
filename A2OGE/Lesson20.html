Task 08

Question Tags (Verification)

We use Question Tags at the end of a statement to ask for confirmation. We are checking if the other person agrees with us.

The Golden Rule: Opposites Attract

Positive Statement ➔ Negative Tag
Negative Statement ➔ Positive Tag

✅ "This commercial is very funny, isn’t it?"

✅ "You don’t like that brand, do you?"

💡 Action Verbs: If the statement uses a normal action verb (like play, buy, go), we use do/does/did in the tag.
"She bought the shoes, didn’t she?"

Task 10

The Psychology of Color

Have you ever noticed that fast-food restaurants often use the colors red and yellow in their logos? This is not an accident.

Psychologists say that the color red makes people feel hungry and energetic, while yellow grabs your attention quickly. If McDonald's had chosen blue and green for their logo, people wouldn’t have felt the same excitement or hunger when they saw it. Blue is usually used by banks because it makes people feel trust and security.

Every detail in a commercial is carefully chosen to manipulate our feelings.

Task 11

Third Conditional (Review)

Just like we learned in Lesson 19, we use the Third Conditional to imagine how a past situation could have been different.

If + Past Perfect (had + V3) , would have + Past Participle (V3)

✅ "If they had chosen a better slogan, they would have sold more products."

✅ "If I had seen the commercial, I would have bought the shoes."
