Task 08

The Past Perfect (had + V3)

We use the Past Perfect to make it clear that one action happened before another action in the past.

Formula & Structure

Subject + had + Past Participle (V3)

✅ "When the astronauts arrived at the viewing pad, the rocket had already launched."

(Action 1: The rocket launched. Action 2: The astronauts arrived. Past Perfect shows which one was first).

[Had launched]
(Earlier Past)
━━━━▶
[Arrived]
(Past)
━━━━▶
[Now]
(Present)

Task 11

Second Conditional (Review)

Just like we learned in Lesson 15, we use the Second Conditional to imagine unreal, impossible, or unlikely situations in the present or future.

If + Past Simple , would + Base Verb (V1)

✅ "If humans went to Mars, we would need to build greenhouses."

✅ "If there were no gravity, we would float away."
