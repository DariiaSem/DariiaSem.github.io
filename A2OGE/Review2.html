Task 4 · Grammar Review

Relative Clauses & Used To

  • 1. He is the influencer (who / which) went viral yesterday.
  • 2. This is the gallery (where / whose) the Mona Lisa is kept.
  • 3. I (used to / use to) buy fast fashion, but now I shop sustainably.
  • 4. Did you (used to / use to) listen to rock music when you were a teenager?
✨
Task 5 · Grammar Review

So/Such & Adjective Order

  • 1. It was (so / such a) trendy outfit that everyone complimented it.
  • 2. The gravity on Mars is (so / such) different from Earth.
  • 3. Order the adjectives: She wore a (cotton / beautiful / red) dress.
  • 4. Order the adjectives: He bought an (old / expensive / Italian) painting.
🔎
Task 6 · Grammar Review

Modals of Deduction

Use must be, might be, or can't be.

  • 1. You've been studying the Mars mission all night! You exhausted. (100% true)
  • 2. That his handwriting. He always writes in cursive, and this is printed. (0% true)
  • 3. The rocket launch delayed because of the weather, but I'm not sure. (50% true)

Task 7 · Grammar Review

2nd & 3rd Conditionals

  • 2nd Conditional (Unreal Present): If we (protect) their habitat, those animals wouldn't be endangered.
  • 2nd Conditional (Unreal Present): If I were an astronaut, I (travel) to Mars.
  • 3rd Conditional (Unreal Past): If he had studied harder, he (graduate) with honors last year.
  • 3rd Conditional (Unreal Past): If the commercial hadn't been so annoying, I (buy) the product.
