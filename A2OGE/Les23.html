Task 2 · Vocabulary Discovery

Disaster Vocabulary

🫨

1. Earthquake

A sudden violent shaking of the ground, causing great destruction.

The earthquake destroyed several buildings in the city center.

🌊

2. Flood

An overflow of a large amount of water beyond its normal limits.

Heavy rain caused a massive flood in the valley.

🚁

3. Rescue

To save someone from a dangerous or difficult situation.

The firefighters managed to rescue the family from the burning house.

⛺

4. Survive

To continue to live or exist, especially in spite of danger.

You need food, water, and shelter to survive in the wild.

🌀

5. Hurricane

A severe storm with high winds that forms over the ocean.

The hurricane is expected to hit the coast on Friday.

🏃

6. Evacuate

To leave a place of danger for a safer place.

The police ordered everyone to evacuate the building immediately.

Task 7 · Grammar Theory 1

Prepositions of Place (in, on, at, under)

These prepositions help us explain exactly where someone or something is located.

PrepositionUsed ForExamples
IN Inside a 3D space, cities, countries in a building, in London, in my bag, in a car
ON Touching a flat surface, public transport on the floor, on the roof, on a train, on the table
AT Specific point/location, events, institutions at the shelter, at home, at a concert, at school
UNDER Directly below or covered by something under the table, under the bed, under water

Task 9 · Grammar Theory 2

Prepositions of Time (Review)

Don't forget the rules from our last lesson! You need both Time and Place to survive.

⏳ Quick Reminder
✅ IN: Months (in May), Years (in 2026), Parts of day (in the morning)
✅ ON: Days (on Friday), Dates (on July 4th)
✅ AT: Exact Times (at 8:00 AM), Night (at night)

Emergency Call

Dispatcher
