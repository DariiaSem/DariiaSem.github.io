Task 08

The Third Conditional (Unreal Past)

We use the Third Conditional to talk about impossible situations in the past. It is often used to express regrets or to imagine how history could have been different.

Formula & Structure

If + Past Perfect (had + V3) , would have + Past Participle (V3)

✅ Reality: She didn't study hard, so she failed.

✅ Third Conditional: "If she had studied hard, she would have passed."

✅ Reality: I woke up late, so I missed the train.

✅ Third Conditional: "If I had woken up early, I would not have missed the train."

Task 10

Song Analysis: Ocean Drive

Let us analyze the themes and lyrics of Duke Dumont's famous track, Ocean Drive.

The Meaning: Ocean Drive is a song about escapism and relationship struggles. It describes two people driving late at night, focusing on the road to avoid having a difficult conversation about their failing romance.

"We're riding down the boulevard
We're riding into the dark
Night, night
With the radio on"

Key Phrases:
- Boulevard: A wide city street, often lined with trees or bright lights.
- Riding into the dark: A metaphor for driving into the unknown, ignoring the reality of their problems.

(To read the complete lyrics, you can search for "Duke Dumont Ocean Drive lyrics" on Google).
