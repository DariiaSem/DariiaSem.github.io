Task 08

Reported Speech (Statements Review)

Let us quickly review how to report what someone said in the past. We usually shift the tense exactly one step backwards.

Direct SpeechReported Speech
He said: I am tired. (Present) He said he was tired. (Past)
She said: I visited the gallery. (Past) She said she had visited the gallery. (Past Perfect)
He told me: I will buy it. (Future) He told me he would buy it. (Conditional)

Task 10

Reported Questions

Reporting a question is a bit different from reporting a statement. We must change the question word order back to normal statement order (Subject + Verb).

1. Wh- Questions

Keep the question word (Where, What, Who), but change the order and tense.

Direct: Where is the gallery?
Reported: She asked where the gallery was.

2. Yes/No Questions

If there is no question word, we use if or whether.

Direct: Do you like art?
Reported: He asked if I liked art.

Task 13

The Stolen Masterpiece

A famous painting was stolen last night. The detective is interviewing the museum director. Roleplay the conversation!

The Detective

Ask direct questions about the crime. "Where were you last night?" "Did you lock the gallery?"

Grammar Target: You will need to report these questions later to the police chief.

The Museum Director

You are very nervous. Answer the questions, but use reported speech to explain what other people told you. "The security guard told me he had locked the doors."
