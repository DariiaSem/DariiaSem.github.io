Task 04

Grammar Rules: The Present Continuous

We use the Present Continuous to talk about actions happening right now, temporary situations, and developing trends.

Structure TypeFormulaExamples
Affirmative (+) Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing The patient is resting.
We are monitoring his vitals.
Negative (-) Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing He is not breathing properly.
They are not working today.
Question (?) Am/Is/Are + Subject + verb-ing ? Is she covering the shift?
Are you feeling better?
⚠️ Usage Triggers: Look for time markers such as currently, at the moment, right now, this week, and nowadays.
Task 05

State Verbs Exception

Certain verbs describe states, not actions. We usually do NOT use them in the continuous form, even if they are happening right now. Decide if these sentences are True (correct) or False (incorrect).

1. The patient is needing assistance right now.
False. Need is a state verb. Say: The patient needs assistance right now.
2. I am understanding the new ward protocol.
False. Understand is a state verb. Say: I understand the protocol.
3. The doctor is assessing the injury.
True. Assess is an action verb.
4. This medication is belonging to Bed 4.
False. Belong is a state verb. Say: This medication belongs to Bed 4.
