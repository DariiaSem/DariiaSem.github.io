Task 04

States vs. Routines

To sound like an Advanced speaker, you must perfectly control the difference between permanent routines (Simple) and temporary states/trends (Continuous).

1. Present Simple (The Ward Reality)

Use it for permanent facts, timetables, and fixed protocols in the hospital.

"The morning shift starts at 07:00. We usually do the handover in 15 minutes."

2. Present Continuous (The London Vibe)

Use it for temporary situations, ongoing trends, or actions happening around now.

"I am staying in an Airbnb in Camden until I find a flat. Rent prices are increasing like crazy!"

Task 08

Perfect vs. Perfect Continuous

The biggest difference between B2 and C1 is mastering the nuance of the Perfect forms. Focus on Result vs. Duration.

Present Perfect Simple

Focuses on the Result or completion. "How much / How many".

"I have triaged 15 patients this morning." (Result)

Present Perfect Continuous

Focuses on the Duration or action itself. "How long".

"I have been viewing flats all week." (Duration)
