TenseNegative AuxiliaryQuestion AuxiliaryBase Verb Change?
Present Simple

Concept Check

Decide if these grammatical statements are True or False.

1. In Present Simple questions with he/she/it, we use "does" and keep the "s" on the main verb.

2. The auxiliary verb for Past Simple negatives is "was not" for action verbs.

True — Are + subject + verb-ing.
