Task 01

The Reunion

We have not seen each other for fourteen days. Select a tile below and let us start talking.

How have you been feeling since our last lesson?
Did you manage to find any time to relax?
What was the most surprising thing that happened to you recently?
Did you travel anywhere new?
Have you watched or read anything interesting?
How is your sleep schedule holding up?
Task 02

Highs and Lows

Every week has its peaks and valleys. Tell me about yours.

The Peak

Tell me about the absolute best moment of the past two weeks. Why was it so good?

The Valley

Tell me about the most difficult or frustrating moment. How did you handle it?

Task 03

Ward Stories

Hospital life never stops. Give me an update on your medical work.

  • What has been happening at the hospital recently?
  • Have you encountered any difficult or unusual cases?
  • How is the dynamic with the senior staff right now?
Task 04

Surviving London

City life can be exhausting. How are you navigating it?

Are the transport systems treating you well, or have you faced delays?
Have you discovered any hidden gems or nice spots in your neighbourhood?
How are you managing the high cost of living recently?
Have you experienced any crazy London weather this week?
Task 05

Agree or Disagree

Read the statements below. Do you agree or disagree? Defend your opinion.

1. Living in London is exhausting but entirely worth it.
Tell me exactly why you feel this way.
2. Night shifts are actually better than day shifts.
What makes one shift better than the other for you?
3. The medical system here is better organized than back home.
Provide specific examples to back up your view.
Task 06

The Energy Audit

A quick check-in on your physical and mental battery levels.

On a scale of one to ten, how physically tired are you today? Why?
↓
On a scale of one to ten, how mentally motivated are you right now?
↓
What is the one thing that drains your energy the most?
↓
What is the one thing that recharges your battery the fastest?
Task 07

Cultural Differences

Observation is key to adaptation. Discuss these cultural points.

  • Have you noticed any weird or interesting British habits over the last two weeks?
  • How do patients interact with doctors here compared to Ukraine?
  • Is there anything you miss terribly about the culture back home?
Task 08

The Venting Room

Sometimes you just need to complain. This is a safe space.

Let it out

Complain to me about something that seriously annoyed you recently. It can be a person, a situation, or a system. I will listen and agree with you.

Task 09

Hypothetical Situations

Let us use our imagination to change reality for a moment.

If you could change one single rule at your hospital, what would it be?
If you had unlimited money to live anywhere in London, which area would you choose?
If you did not have to work tomorrow, how exactly would you spend the day?
If you could instantly learn one new medical skill perfectly, what would it be?
Task 10

The Food Diary

Food is fuel, but it is also a huge part of living in a new place.

  • What is the best thing you ate over the last two weeks?
  • Have you tried any traditional British pub food recently?
  • How is your meal prep going? Are you finding time to cook?
Task 11

Patient Interactions

Communication in healthcare is unpredictable.

Storytime

Tell me a story about a memorable patient interaction you had recently. It can be funny, frustrating, or heartwarming. Walk me through exactly what happened.

Task 12

Advice For The Instructor

Role reversal. Your instructor has a problem, and you need to solve it.

Instructor: I have been feeling completely burnt out and unmotivated to do my daily tasks.
↓
You: Ask follow-up questions to diagnose the root of my problem.
↓
Instructor: I am working too many hours and not sleeping enough.
↓
You: Give me strict, actionable advice on how to fix this situation immediately.

Task 14

Goal Setting

Looking ahead to the next two weeks.

  • What is your number one priority at work for the next fourteen days?
  • What is one personal goal you want to achieve outside of the hospital?
  • How can we structure our future English lessons to best support these goals?
Task 15

Open Floor

This space is yours. Is there absolutely anything else you want to talk about today?

Catch Up Complete! ✨

It was fantastic to hear about your week. We will return to strict grammar drills next time!

🏠 Homework

Self-Reflection Journal

Take your time to write detailed answers to these fifteen reflective prompts. No right or wrong answers today, just pure expression.

1. Write a short paragraph about the best thing that happened this week.
2. Describe a difficult interaction you had recently and how you handled it.
3. What is one thing you learned about nursing in the UK recently?
4. What is one thing you miss the most about your home country right now?
5. Write down three new English words you heard outside of class this week.
6. Describe your favourite spot in London so far.
7. If you could change one decision you made last week, what would it be?
8. What is the most exhausting part of your current routine?
9. Write about a moment you felt genuinely proud of your medical skills.
10. How do you plan to relax this coming weekend?
11. What is one British food you actually enjoy eating?
12. Describe a goal you want to achieve before the end of the month.
13. Who is the most interesting person you spoke to recently?
14. What advice would you give to someone moving to London tomorrow?
15. Do you feel your English has improved over the past month? Why?
