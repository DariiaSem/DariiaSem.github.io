Task 04

Grammar Rules: The Ultimate Division

The fundamental difference in English lies between what is permanent and what is temporary.

Present Simple

Use for: Facts, Routines, Permanent States.

  • Water boils at 100 degrees. (Fact)
  • I take the Tube every day. (Routine)
  • I live in London. (Permanent)

Present Continuous

Use for: Right now, Temporary Situations, Trends.

  • Prices are skyrocketing this year. (Trend)
  • I am taking the bus this week. (Temporary)
  • I am living in a hostel right now. (Temporary)
Task 05

The State Verb Exception

Even if something is happening exactly at this moment, you CANNOT use the continuous form if the verb describes a state of mind, possession, or a sense.

CategoryCommon State VerbsCorrect Usage
Thoughts & Feelings Know, believe, understand, want, prefer, like, hate. I understand the problem now.
Not: I am understanding.
Possession Have, own, belong. This flat belongs to my friend.
Not: This flat is belonging.
Senses Seem, sound, smell, taste. This coffee tastes terrible.
Not: This coffee is tasting.
