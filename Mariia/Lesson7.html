Task 04

Grammar Rules: Past Continuous

The Past Continuous describes actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past, or actions that were interrupted by another event.

Past Continuous

Use for: Actions in progress at a specific past time, setting the scene.

  • At 3 AM, I was monitoring the patient. (In progress)
  • It was raining, and the ward was quiet. (Setting the scene)
  • Structure: Subject + was/were + verb-ing.

Past Continuous vs Past Simple

Use for: Interrupted actions.

  • I was writing the report when the alarm rang.
  • Continuous = long action (background).
  • Simple = short action (interruption).
