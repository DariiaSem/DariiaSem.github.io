Task 05

Grammar Rules: The Past Simple

The Past Simple describes actions that started and completely finished in the past. It is the primary tense for narrative storytelling.

Structure TypeFormulaExamples
Affirmative (+) Subject + V2 (Verb + ed / Irregular) I worked late yesterday.
She found the documents.
Negative (-) Subject + did not + V1 (Base verb) He did not call me.
We did not go to the meeting.
Question (?) Did + Subject + V1 (Base verb) ? Did you finish the report?
Where did they travel?
⚠️ Critical Rule: In negative sentences and questions, the auxiliary verb did takes the past tense. The main verb must remain in its base form (V1). Never write did not went.
Task 06

Pronunciation Lab: -ed Endings

Regular verbs end in -ed, but there are three completely different ways to pronounce this ending. Click the audio buttons to hear the difference.

Sound 1: /t/ (After unvoiced sounds like k, p, f, s, ch, sh).
Example: work → worked.
Sound 2: /d/ (After voiced sounds like l, v, n, m, r, b, v, g, w, y, z and vowels).
Example: play → played.
Sound 3: /ɪd/ (ONLY after verbs ending in T or D). This adds a completely new syllable.
Example: want → wanted.
