Task 07

Mixed Conditionals Type 3+2

In high level corporate banking we rarely use simple hypothetical structures. We constantly map how a past error directly impacts our present reality[cite: 12].

Past Action causing Present Consequence

Formula: If + Past Perfect , would/could + bare infinitive.

Fact: We delegated everything to AI yesterday. We are bankrupt today.

Hypothesis: If we had not delegated everything to AI we would not be bankrupt today.

Task 12

Pronunciation Lab

When native speakers deliver hypothetical consequences they heavily compress the auxiliary verbs. Practice the reductions below.

The Auxiliary Compression

Would have
Sounds like: Woulda /wʊdə/
The H and the V are entirely dropped.
Would not have
Sounds like: Wouldnt of /wʊdntəv/
The T and H blend together seamlessly.
If it had not
Sounds like: If it hadnt /ɪf ɪt hædnt/
Deliver this clause incredibly fast to place the stress on the main verb.
