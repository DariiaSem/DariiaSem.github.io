Task 04

Conditionals Matrix

The complete breakdown of conditional structures. Notice how the tenses shift backwards to express unreality.

TypeIf Clause (Condition)Main Clause (Result)Use Case
Type 0 Present Simple
(If you heat water...)		 Present Simple
(...it boils.)		 Universal truths, scientific facts, absolute clinic rules.
Type 1 Present Simple
(If she asks for a raise...)		 will or can plus V1
(...the boss will agree.)		 Real, possible situations in the present or future.
Type 2 Past Simple
(If I owned this clinic...)		 would or could plus V1
(...I would change the software.)		 Unreal, hypothetical, or highly unlikely present/future situations.
Type 3 Past Perfect
(If he had checked the schedule...)		 would have plus V3
(...he would not have missed the patient.)		 Unreal past. Regrets or analyzing things that did not happen.
Mixed A Past Perfect
(If I had studied medicine...)		 would plus V1
(...I would be a doctor now.)		 Past condition with a Present result.
Mixed B Past Simple
(If she were not so ambitious...)		 would have plus V3
(...she would not have accepted the role.)		 Present condition with a Past result.
⚠ Golden Rule: Never put will or would immediately after the word if. Use the correct tense instead.

Task 07

Reported Speech: The Full Audit

When we report what someone said in the past, three things usually change. Tense, Pronouns, and Time expressions.

The Backshift Rule

If the reporting verb is in the past, the tense of the original statement shifts one step back into the past.

  • Present Simple becomes Past Simple. Direct Speech: I want a raise. Reported Speech: She said she wanted a raise.
  • Present Continuous becomes Past Continuous. Direct Speech: I am leaving. Reported Speech: He said he was leaving.
  • Past Simple or Present Perfect becomes Past Perfect. Direct Speech: I finished. Reported Speech: She said she had finished.
  • Will becomes Would. Direct Speech: I will try. Reported Speech: He said he would try.

Reporting Questions and Commands

Questions lose their auxiliary verbs and revert to normal sentence order. Commands use the infinitive.

  • Wh Questions: Direct Speech: Where is the file? Reported Speech: He asked me where the file was.
  • Yes or No Questions: Direct Speech: Are you tired? Reported Speech: She asked me if I was tired.
  • Commands: Direct Speech: Sterilize these instruments. Reported Speech: He told me to sterilize the instruments.
