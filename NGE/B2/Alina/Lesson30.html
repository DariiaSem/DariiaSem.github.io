Task 07

Storytelling Syntax: Hooks

When recounting events from a busy clinic shift, C2 speakers use specific grammatical structures to build tension, emphasize emotions, and hook the listener.

1. Cleft Sentences (for Emphasis)

Instead of "The doctor's attitude was annoying," we split (cleave) the sentence to emphasize a specific part.

  • What struck me most was the sheer volume of patients on Monday.
  • The thing I found most challenging was dealing with the broken autoclave.
  • It wasn't until the end of the shift that I finally relaxed.

2. Negative Inversion (for Drama)

Placing a negative word at the start of the sentence for dramatic effect. Remember to invert the subject and auxiliary verb!

  • Little did I know that the procedure would take five hours. (I didn't know at all)
  • Not only was the patient uncooperative, but he also refused the anesthetic.
  • Never before had I seen such a disorganized schedule.
